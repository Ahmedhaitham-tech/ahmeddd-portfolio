Our story

Built around a long table and a slower clock.

Emberwood began as a supper club in a converted carpenter's workshop — walnut beams overhead, a wood-fired oven pulled from an old bakery, and one long table where strangers ended the night as regulars.

Today the room is a little bigger, but the idea hasn't moved an inch. Warm plates, honest cooking, wines from growers we've eaten dinner with, and a fire that stays lit from open to last call.

Come hungry. Stay curious. Leave later than you meant to.
