Our story

Butter, flour, patience — and time.

Farine & Foil started as a tiny home oven and a stubborn belief that a croissant deserves 72 hours of care. We source stone-milled flour, seasonal Egyptian fruit, and small-farm butter, and we let the dough decide when it's ready.

Every loaf is scored by hand. Every tart is glazed before it leaves the marble. Nothing here is quick — and that's the point.

"We don't chase trends. We chase the smell of a good crust at 6 a.m."
— Head Baker, Farine & Foil
