What we can help with

Every plan starts with an exam. You get the options and the cost in writing before any treatment begins.

  • Check-up and clean

    A full exam, a digital scan and a gentle polish. We show you what we see.

    45 minutes

  • Whitening

    Supervised whitening that is matched to your enamel, with a shade guide so you can pick the result.

    60 minutes

  • Clear aligners

    A scan, a 3D preview of the finished smile, and regular check-ins while you wear them.

    30 minute consult

  • Implants

    A single tooth or several, planned with a scan and explained step by step.

    45 minute consult

  • Emergency care

    Toothache, a chipped tooth or a lost filling. Call in the morning and we will fit you in the same day.

    Same day

Your first visit, step by step

Most first visits take about an hour. Here is how they go.

  1. Talk

    We start with what is bothering you, not a form.

  2. Look

  3. Plan

    You get the options and the cost before anything begins.

  4. Treat

    Treatment happens at your pace, with breaks whenever you ask.
